# BLW Field Guide

Use this file to keep BLW notes consistent across recipes.

This is not medical advice. It is a formatting guide for family recipe pages.

## Required BLW fields

### Baby portion

What part of the family meal can the baby eat?

### Set aside before

List the cooking steps or ingredients that should happen after the baby portion is removed.

Common examples:

```text
salt
spicy seasoning
honey
sweet glaze
high-sodium sauce
```

### Texture target

Describe the physical texture.

Example:

```text
Soft enough to mash between fingers.
```

### Cut or shape guidance

Describe how to serve each baby-appropriate component.

Example:

```text
Broccoli: very soft florets with enough stem to hold.
Potato: soft wedges or large mashable pieces.
Chicken: tender strips or shredded pieces.
```

### Allergen notes

List relevant allergens in the recipe.

### Choking-risk flags

Call out items that must be modified, omitted, or softened.

## Do not over-document

Keep BLW notes practical. A recipe page should help the family cook dinner, not become a medical reference.
