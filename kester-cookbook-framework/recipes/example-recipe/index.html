Example Recipe

A simple test recipe for checking AnyList browser-extension import.

Servings: 4

Prep time: 10 minutes

Cook time: 20 minutes

Total time: 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 cup uncooked rice
  • 2 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

Instructions

  1. Prepare the chicken, rice, and broccoli.
  2. Cook the chicken until browned and cooked through.
  3. Cook the rice according to package directions.
  4. Steam the broccoli until tender.
  5. Serve the chicken with rice and broccoli.
